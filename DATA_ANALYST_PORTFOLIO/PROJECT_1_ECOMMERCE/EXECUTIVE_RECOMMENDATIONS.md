# EXECUTIVE RECOMMENDATIONS - Project 1

### 1. Revenue Performance
- Total Revenue: INR 6.99 Crore

- Average Order Value: INR 13,987

### 2. Customer Segments
- Champions: 264 customers (26.5%) - Highest value
- At Risk: 156 customers (15.7%) - Need re-engagement

- Total Leakage: INR 37.09 Lakh (5.30%)

## Recommended Actions

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. Fix Payment Gateway
- **Problem:** 248 orders have payment mismatches
- **Impact:** INR 3.02 Lakh at risk
- **Action:** 
  - Investigate payment processing errors
  - Implement payment validation rules
  - Add real-time payment verification

#### 2. Reduce Refunds
- **Problem:** 500 refunds totaling INR 34.07 Lakh
- **Impact:** 4.87% refund rate
- **Action:**
  - Improve product descriptions
  - Add quality checks before shipping
  - Implement return reason tracking

### MEDIUM PRIORITY

#### 3. Improve Onboarding
- **Problem:** Month 1 retention only 20.6%
- **Impact:** Customer acquisition cost wasted
- **Action:**
  - Create welcome email series
  - Offer first-purchase discount
  - Implement progress tracking

#### 4. Re-engage At Risk Customers
- **Problem:** 156 customers inactive for 500+ days
- **Impact:** Potential INR 61.9 Lakh revenue
- **Action:**
  - Send personalized email campaigns
  - Offer comeback discounts
  - Survey for feedback

### LOW PRIORITY

#### 5. Review Bottom Products
- **Problem:** 13 products generate less than INR 20K each
- **Impact:** Inventory space wasted
- **Action:**
  - Analyze sales trends
  - Consider discontinuation
  - Or increase marketing
